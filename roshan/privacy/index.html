Roshan — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

The short version. Roshan does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no adverts and no analytics. The app works entirely offline.

Information we collect

None. Roshan does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. We have no servers and no database.

Information stored on your device

While you are using the app, your scores for the current session are held in the device's temporary memory so the app can show your progress screen. This information is erased as soon as you close the app. It is never written to permanent storage, never leaves your device, and is never visible to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

Roshan requests no Android permissions. It does not use the internet, the camera, the microphone, location, storage or contacts.

Health information

Roshan does not collect, store or transmit any health information. The activities in the app are general wellbeing exercises. Nothing you do in the app is recorded or reported anywhere.

Children

Roshan is designed for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Third parties

Roshan contains no advertising networks, no analytics software and no third-party trackers. No data is shared with anyone, because no data is collected.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
