# Privacy policy

Google Play requires a privacy policy at a **public URL that loads without a login**,
even for apps that collect nothing.

A ready-to-host page is at `privacy/index.html` in this repo. Push it to a GitHub
repository, turn on Pages under Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → `main` → `/root`,
and the URL will be:

```
https://<username>.github.io/<repo>/privacy/
```

Paste that into the Play Console store listing and into the Data safety section.

**Before publishing, replace `CONTACT_EMAIL_HERE` in `privacy/index.html`** with a real
address the group monitors. A policy with no working contact is a rejection risk, and
Play shows the address publicly, so use a project address rather than a personal one if
the group prefers.

Update the "Last updated" date whenever the policy changes.

## Policy text

**Roshan — Privacy Policy**

Last updated: 28 October 2026

**The short version.** Roshan does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts,
no sign-up, no adverts and no analytics. The app works entirely offline.

**Information we collect.** None. Roshan does not ask for your name, email address,
phone number, date of birth, location, contacts, photos or any other personal
information. We have no servers and no database.

**Information stored on your device.** While you are using the app, your scores for the
current session are held in the device's temporary memory so the app can show your
progress screen. This information is erased as soon as you close the app. It is never
written to permanent storage, never leaves your device, and is never visible to us or to
anyone else.

**Permissions.** Roshan requests no Android permissions. It does not use the internet,
the camera, the microphone, location, storage or contacts.

**Health information.** Roshan does not collect, store or transmit any health
information. The activities in the app are general wellbeing exercises. Nothing you do
in the app is recorded or reported anywhere.

**Children.** Roshan is designed for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

**Third parties.** Roshan contains no advertising networks, no analytics software and no
third-party trackers. No data is shared with anyone, because no data is collected.

**Changes to this policy.** If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted
on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

**Contact.** Questions about this policy can be sent to CONTACT_EMAIL_HERE.

**A note on the app's purpose.** Roshan offers cognitive practice activities for general
wellbeing. It does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition and is not a substitute for
assessment and treatment by a qualified occupational therapist or doctor.
